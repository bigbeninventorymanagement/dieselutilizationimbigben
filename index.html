Monthly utilization report

Per-unit consumption for the month, measured fill-to-fill against the previous meter reading. Variance compares actual rate with the unit's standard.

Rate uses only fills with a valid previous reading. Rates are liters per km (odometer) or liters per hour (hour meter). With a unit standard: Over more than 15% above it, Watch 5–15% above, Good within. Without one, the default max rate in Tank settings decides Good or Over.
